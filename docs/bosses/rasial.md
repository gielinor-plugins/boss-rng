# Rasial, the First Necromancer

Source: [RuneScape Wiki](https://runescape.wiki/w/Rasial,_the_First_Necromancer) (CC BY-NC-SA 3.0), checked 29 October 2026.

## Drop rates

Each kill makes two rolls at the same time, so a kill can rarely give two:

| Roll | Rate | Each unique |
|---|---|---|
| Weapon: Omni guard or Soulbound lantern | 1 in 320 | 1 in 640 |
| Armor: one of five First Necromancer pieces | 1 in 128 | 1 in 640 |

## How kills are counted

- A solo fight.

## Vars read

| Var | Holds |
|---|---|
| `BSLAY_KILLS_RASIAL_BOSS` | Kill count. |
| `ITEMLOG_ITEM_NECRO_RASIAL_<PIECE>_T95` | Boss log count each: `ARMGUARD` (Omni guard), `LANTERN`, `HEAD`, `BODY`, `LEGS`, `HANDS`, `FEET`. |

## Not counted

- **Miso** (pet): pets aren't counted.
- **Rasial's crown** (boss trophy, 1 in 500): not on the collection log.

| Boss log item | Id |
|---|---|
| Miso | 55672 |
