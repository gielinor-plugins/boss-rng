# Your data and privacy

## What Boss RNG reads

Only information the game already shows you:

- **Kill counts and collection log counts,** as the boss log and collection log
  show them, without either being open.
- **What sets a kill's drop rates,** such as Amascut's enrage, Croesus contribution,
  the Barrows brothers killed, the players in an elite dungeon, or whether your
  pontifex shadow ring is overcharged. Each boss's page in
  [Supported bosses](../bosses/README.md) lists every value it reads.
- **Your loot chests and loot piles,** for the bosses counted by them (Vorkath
  chests opened, Kerapac loot piles, Amascut and Sanctum of Rebirth claims).
- **The game's date,** so each kill uses the drop rates in effect that day.
- **Your character's display name,** to keep each character's data apart.

It never reads hidden values, such as bad luck mitigation or loot waiting before
you claim it, and never acts in the game for you.

## What it saves

The kills, uniques and dry streaks it counts, and some of the values that set their
drop rates, saved on your computer under your character's display name. A name
change starts a new entry. The game's own counts are read each time, never saved.
[Saved data](../data/saved-data.md) describes the exact format.

## What leaves your computer

Nothing. Boss RNG makes no internet requests, requests no plugin permissions, and
sends nothing to anyone, including the developer. Other plugins can't read its
data.

## Deleting your data

Data Controls in Boss RNG's settings (Plugin Hub, Manage tab) resets one boss or
every boss for the character you're logged in as.

Since nothing is sent anywhere, the developer holds no data about you. Questions
about your data are still welcome at gielinorplugins@gmail.com.
